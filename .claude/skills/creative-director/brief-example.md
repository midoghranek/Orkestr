# Example brief (kafeeh acceptance project)

Source of truth: `scripts/seed-kafeeh.ts` (`npm run seed:kafeeh`; needs the sibling CafeOS repo, or `KAFEEH_REPO`).

- Brand written **Kafeeh**, pronounced in narration **«كافيه»** (`settings.brandPronunciation`). Campaign line **«كافيهك تحت سيطرتك»**.
- Language: Egyptian Arabic narration and on-screen text; RTL layout; typeface IBM Plex Sans Arabic.
- Palette: forest `#1A3324`/`#22412D`, cream `#F7F1E8`, terracotta `#C97A55`.
- Photos in the library are crops from GENERATED concept boards: placeholders. Replace with real Egyptian cafe footage before a paid campaign, and keep `draftBanner` on until then.
- Verified claims are the 19 strings in `CLAIMS` (14-day free trial, offline cash orders, shift close, audit log ...). Pricing is undecided: never state a price.

Brief text:

> اعمل إعلان لـ Kafeeh (بيتنطق «كافيه») — نظام كاشير وتشغيل للكافيهات في مصر. الفكرة: «كافيهك تحت سيطرتك».
> Deliverables: 30-35 s 16:9 commercial, 15 s 9:16 social cut, 16:9 desktop homepage loop, 9:16 mobile homepage loop, plus posters.
> Audience: Egyptian cafe owners and managers. Language: Egyptian Arabic narration and on-screen text.
> Story: the busy rush -> Kafeeh on an ordinary tablet/phone keeps it in order -> internet cuts and the cashier keeps selling cash -> end of shift, expected vs counted is clear -> 14-day free trial, no payment details.
> Use real Kafeeh UI captured from the live site, the official logo and the brand photography. Only use the verified claims.

Name the deliverables so `planToGraph` classifies them: "Commercial 16:9" (cut), "Social cut 9:16" (cut, 15 s: built without the 32 s VO), "Homepage loop desktop" (loop), "Poster 4:5" (poster).
